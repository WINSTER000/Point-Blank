Frequently Asked Questions

How does firearm purchasing work at Point Blank?

All firearm purchases are shipped to a licensed FFL dealer. The dealer will complete the required background check and finalize paperwork before legally taking possession.

What are the requirements to buy a firearm?

Buyers must be at least 21 years old, present a valid government-issued photo ID, pass a federal background check, and comply with all applicable state and local firearm laws.

How long does Point Blank take to ship orders?

Orders are processed within one to three business days after purchase. Shipping time to your selected FFL dealer typically ranges between three and seven business days.

What gunsmithing services does Point Blank offer?

Point Blank’s certified gunsmiths provide professional services including firearm cleaning, repairs, upgrades, customization, and performance modifications.
